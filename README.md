# Smart_AI_Retail_System

### From fragmented retail data to governed analytics, decision intelligence and AI-powered retail operations

The **Smart AI Retail System**is an end-to-end **Data, Business Intelligence and AI Engineering project**designed to transform raw retail sales, inventory and promotional data into a governed decision-support platform.

The project begins with real-world operational datasets containing sales performance, stock-on-hand and Sell-Out Allowance (SOA) information and progressively builds a scalable analytical architecture covering:

**Data Engineering → Data Modelling → Business Intelligence → Inventory Intelligence → Forecasting → Pricing Intelligence → AI-Assisted Retail Operations**

The objective is not simply to build dashboards.

The long-term goal is to demonstrate how a traditional retail reporting environment can evolve into an **AI-enabled retail decision system**capable of helping management and store teams answer questions such as:

* What is selling?

* Where is inventory concentrated?

* Which products are generating the most revenue and profit?

* Which SKUs are overstocked, understocked or commercially at risk?

* What products should be reordered?

* How should competitor pricing and promotional allowances influence pricing decisions?
* Can staff retrieve reliable product knowledge instantly instead of searching through manuals and websites?

## 🎯 Project Vision

Traditional retail operations often rely on disconnected spreadsheets, manually generated reports and individual staff knowledge.
This creates several problems:

- fragmented sales and inventory information
- inconsistent product identifiers
- limited visibility of store-level stock
- manual KPI calculation
- slow identification of overstock and stock risks
- limited pricing intelligence
- dependence on employee product knowledge
- difficult integration of AI because the underlying data is not governed

The Smart AI Retail System addresses this by building a **single governed analytical foundation** that can support both human decision-makers and downstream AI systems.

# 💼 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large amounts of operational data, but having data does not automatically create useful decisions.

Sales information, stock reports and promotional allowances may exist in different structures and at different levels of granularity.

For example, this project works with:

| Data Source                   | Business Purpose                                                          |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Master_Sales_data.xlsx`    | Product/category sales, revenue, cost, profit and stock-level information |
| `Stock_on_Hand_Report.xlsx` | Store-level inventory across multiple retail locations                    |
| `MasterSOA.xlsx`            | Sell-Out Allowance promotional values and validity windows                |


The challenge is therefore not only visualization.

The core engineering problem is to  **reconcile these operational datasets into a reliable analytical model before using them for business decisions or AI** .

# 💰 Pricing Intelligence

A major future extension of the system is a  **competitor-aware pricing recommendation pipeline** .

## 🛡️ Human-in-the-Loop Pricing

The project intentionally does **not** treat autonomous price changing as the default.

# 🏷️ Electronic Shelf Label Automation

Once a pricing recommendation has passed the required approval controls, the future architecture is designed to integrate with an **Electronic Shelf Label (ESL)** platform.

# 🤖 AI Retail Assistant

The final intelligence layer is a grounded  **AI Retail Assistant** .

Instead of allowing an LLM to answer directly from uncontrolled spreadsheets, the assistant will query governed analytical views.

Example management questions could include:

> Which products have strong sales but insufficient inventory?

> Which category generated the highest gross profit?

> Which SKUs should be reviewed for replenishment?

Every analytical answer is intended to remain traceable to the governed data layer.

# 🧠 Product Knowledge Layer

The AI Retail Assistant is also planned to support frontline retail staff.
